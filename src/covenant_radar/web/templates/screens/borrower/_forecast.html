How to read this forecast

Rules: the operational risk score combines distance to the covenant boundary, deterioration and sustained evidence. It is a prioritisation score, not a calibrated probability of covenant breach.

Machine learning: when available, the challenger estimate is shown separately. A shadow model does not change the queue or case priority.

Confidence: describes the support available from the input data. A projected crossing is a scenario based on the stored trend and assumptions, not a guaranteed event.

Generative AI: drafts a separate memo from the stored evidence. It does not calculate the contractual covenant test.
