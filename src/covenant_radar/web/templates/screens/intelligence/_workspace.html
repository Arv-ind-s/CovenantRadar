Method, assumptions and source health

How to read this

  • Quarter so far: the average since the reported quarter ended against that quarter’s average. It is what the next, unreported quarter is absorbing.
  • Latest: the most recent observation against the same base. It is what the following quarter absorbs if prices hold.
  • Dollar-priced commodities are converted to rupees with USD/INR over the same windows. Monthly IMF prices arrive about a month in arrears; each figure shows its period.
  • Input-cost baskets weight each commodity by its assumed share, so a fall in one input can offset a rise in another.
  • Nothing here changes stored ranks, forecasts, bands or covenant results. Record a conclusion in the borrower’s case.
