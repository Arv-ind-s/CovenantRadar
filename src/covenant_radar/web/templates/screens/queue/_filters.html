{{ labels.glossary_title }}
Order
Row 1 is the borrower to look at first. The order weighs the risk score against the size of the exposure and how complete the data is, so a large loan with rising risk sits above a small one.
{{ labels.column_band }}
Act now means a risk score of 70% or more on the covenant most at risk. Amber is 40% to 69%. Watch is below 40%: listed so nothing is missed, but not escalated.
{{ labels.column_dated_risk }}
When the forecast projects a covenant crossing its limit, and the risk score behind that. The score sets priority; it is not a guarantee that a breach will happen. If there is too little reliable data to project, the row says so instead of showing a number.
Next 30, 60 and 90 days
The risk score at each point ahead, shown in a row's detail, so you can see whether risk is building. Where a period has too little reliable data, it is marked as not shown.
{{ labels.column_sma_band }}
The RBI Special Mention Account class from the borrower's repayment record: SMA-0 is 1 to 30 days overdue, SMA-1 is 31 to 60 days, SMA-2 is 61 to 90 days. It is tracked separately from the covenant forecast.
{{ labels.column_what_changed }}
How the borrower's band has moved since the previous review, for example from Watch to Amber. A borrower's first review has nothing to compare against yet.
